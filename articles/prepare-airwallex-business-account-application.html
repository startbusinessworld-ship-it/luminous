Prepare an Airwallex business account application

Start an Airwallex application with a clear explanation of the business.

The practical answer

Start an Airwallex application with a clear explanation of the business. Identify the company, the people behind it and the services you need. Incorporation documents support that application but do not replace the platform's review or establish automatic access to every product.

Keep the distinction clear

Airwallex is a financial platform, not a bank in Hong Kong. Eligibility, product availability and approval depend on its review and current terms. Images of a visit do not by themselves establish a partnership. Airwallex: Hong Kong platform and products.

A practical example

An online seller can explain its products, storefront and supplier relationships together. Airwallex may require additional information before deciding whether the business and requested products are eligible.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Check business and location eligibility.
  2. Prepare company and ownership information.
  3. Describe the intended payment activity accurately.

Can Luminos approve the Airwallex application?

No. Luminos can assist with company setup and application preparation. Airwallex conducts its review and decides approval.
