Your trading company: align contracts and documents

An import-export company should be identifiable throughout its commercial paperwork.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 16-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

An import-export company should be identifiable throughout its commercial paperwork. The entity on purchase orders, customer contracts and invoices should match the actual arrangements. Hong Kong incorporation gives the company a legal identity but does not automatically resolve customs, product or destination-country requirements.

Keep the distinction clear

A company structure, payment arrangements and the rules in the countries where a business operates are separate considerations. Plan them together without assuming that incorporation settles every cross-border question. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

If a company purchases goods from one country and sells to a customer elsewhere, retain the contract chain and payment evidence. Clarify which party is responsible for each part of the trade instead of relying on the word international.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Identify the contracting entity on each side.
  2. Keep purchase and sales records connected.
  3. Check product and destination requirements separately.

Does Hong Kong incorporation remove obligations in the markets I sell into?

No. Product, customs and other local requirements can apply where goods are sourced, moved or sold. Assess the actual trade route.
