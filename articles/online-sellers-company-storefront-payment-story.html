Online sellers: prepare the company, storefront and payment story together

An online store is easier to explain when its products, legal entity and payment arrangements are consistent. Build the explanation around a real customer transaction rather than a collection of platform logos.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

Describe the offer and sales channels

Identify the products, intended buyers and storefronts. Explain whether you hold stock, use a fulfilment partner or follow another model. Be specific about who handles delivery and customer support.

Check the customer-facing information

Make the contracting entity, terms and invoice details understandable. Review refund and contact information. Do not present a payment service as available until its actual setup and approval are complete.

Keep supplier and order evidence connected

Link purchase orders, customer orders, invoices and settlement records. Distinguish product samples and quotations from completed transactions. Keep genuine evidence of how the business operates.

Choose services for the actual model

Ask about supported activities, payment methods and currencies. An account and a checkout processor are different products. Their availability and review requirements may differ.

An online-store consistency check

Offer
Actual products, stock and fulfilment model.
Customer information
Contracting entity, terms and contact details.
Payments
Checkout, settlements and refunds.
Records
Supplier evidence, orders and transaction references.

Follow a typical customer order through the store and back-office records. Check what the buyer sees as well as what the company records. If the store is not yet live, mark the arrangements as planned. Confirm the actual service availability and provider approval before advertising a payment feature; a platform logo alone does not establish that it is ready to use.

Frequently asked questions

Is selling on a platform enough evidence by itself?

A sales channel helps explain the model, but additional ownership, activity and transaction information may be requested.

Can I show projected sales?

Yes when clearly labelled as projections; never present them as completed revenue.
