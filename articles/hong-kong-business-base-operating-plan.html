Hong Kong as a business base: connect the skyline to an operating plan

Hong Kong's skyline represents an international business centre, but choosing a base requires more than an attractive city image. Connect the location to the way your business will actually trade.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article

  2. Check company functions before launch
  3. Treat payments as a separate workstream

Identify the practical connection

Describe the suppliers, customers or services for which the proposed company would be used. Explain whether the company will sell goods, provide services or perform another role. Keep assumptions separate from confirmed relationships.

Check company functions before launch

Plan the registered office, company secretary and access to company records. Identify who monitors correspondence and later filing requests. These arrangements are part of operating the company, not a final decorative step.

Treat payments as a separate workstream

List the currencies, expected counterparties and payment methods. Compare providers against the actual model. No skyline, incorporation document or service package removes the provider's own assessment.

Use a short launch readiness review

Before the first transaction, check the contracting name, invoice details, record storage and payment instructions. Resolve inconsistent information before it reaches a customer or provider.

What must be resolved before proceeding?

For an early-stage business, complete the worksheet with confirmed facts first. Leave an unknown marked as unknown and assign a way to resolve it. For an existing business, use actual contracts and transactions to test whether the current description is still accurate. The worksheet is a discussion aid, not a determination of legal or tax status.

Frequently asked questions

Does a Hong Kong company require me to move there?

A non-resident can be a director, but the company's local functions and any obligations elsewhere still require attention.

Is an international image enough to justify the structure?

No. Document the operational reasons and assess the actual circumstances.
