Airwallex: payments, invoices and overseas suppliers

Airwallex brings several payment functions into a financial platform: eligible businesses can explore online payments, invoice-related tools and international supplier payments.

The practical answer

Airwallex brings several payment functions into a financial platform: eligible businesses can explore online payments, invoice-related tools and international supplier payments. Product access depends on the account, business location and review process. Luminos can help prepare the company setup and application information, while Airwallex decides approval.

Keep the distinction clear

Airwallex is a financial platform, not a bank in Hong Kong. Eligibility, product availability and approval depend on its review and current terms. Images of a visit do not by themselves establish a partnership. Airwallex: Hong Kong platform and products.

A practical example

An online seller may need to collect a customer payment, document the invoice and send money to a supplier. Those are different workflows. Check the relevant products rather than assuming a business account includes every payment feature immediately.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Identify the collection and payout functions you need.
  2. Review eligibility and supported locations.
  3. Prepare the business information for the application.

Is Airwallex a bank?

Airwallex describes itself in Hong Kong as a financial platform, not a bank. Account access and payment features remain subject to its terms and approval.
