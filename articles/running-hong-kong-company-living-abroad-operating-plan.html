Running a Hong Kong company while living abroad: an operating plan

Remaining abroad does not remove the need to organise the local company functions. Build a working arrangement for correspondence, records and decisions before the first filing is due.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

Separate your location from company functions

Hong Kong permits non-resident directors. The company still needs the required Hong Kong registered office and an eligible company secretary. These functions should be arranged explicitly rather than assumed from a service provider's marketing.

Set up a correspondence routine

Decide who receives notices, how they reach the director and who tracks the response. Use a shared deadline record and a named contact. A letter reaching the registered office is not the same as the director dealing with it.

Plan remote access securely

Keep controlled access to company documents and service accounts. Avoid shared passwords in chat messages. Know how to recover access if the main contact is unavailable.

Check the other jurisdictions involved

Living and managing a business elsewhere can raise local questions. Explain your real location and operating pattern when seeking advice; incorporation in Hong Kong does not settle every cross-border obligation.

A remote company coordination plan

Correspondence
Who receives notices and forwards them.
Decisions
Who reviews and approves the next action.
Records
Where current company documents are stored.
Continuity
How an appropriate alternative contact gets access.

Test the plan before a time-sensitive request arrives. Confirm that the director can see the relevant documents and that messages reach the responsible contact. Keep secure access arrangements separate from ordinary chat messages. Remote coordination does not remove local company requirements or determine obligations connected with where people actually work and manage the business.

Frequently asked questions

Do all directors need to live in Hong Kong?

No. A director need not ordinarily reside in Hong Kong.

Does remote incorporation guarantee remote bank opening?

No. The provider sets its own verification and application requirements.
