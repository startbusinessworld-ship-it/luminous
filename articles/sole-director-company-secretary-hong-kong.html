Can a sole director also be the company secretary?

A Hong Kong private company's sole director cannot also act as its company secretary.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 15-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

A Hong Kong private company's sole director cannot also act as its company secretary. Treat management and company-secretarial arrangements as separate decisions. The secretary must also meet the applicable residence or corporate-location requirements; the title is not simply an informal administrative contact.

Keep the distinction clear

Ownership, management and company-secretarial responsibilities have different functions. Record the appointments accurately and check the relevant eligibility rules before filing. Hong Kong Companies Registry: directors and company secretary.

A practical example

A founder may be the only shareholder and director but still needs an eligible secretary. Naming the same founder in all three positions would not solve the company-secretary requirement where that person is the sole director.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Confirm whether there will be one or several directors.
  2. Appoint an eligible company secretary separately.
  3. Check the details before submitting the incorporation forms.

Can an overseas founder be the only director?

A director need not be a Hong Kong resident. The sole-director restriction concerns acting as company secretary as well, rather than the director's nationality.
