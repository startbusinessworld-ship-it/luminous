How to describe your business activity for company preparation

Instead of international trade, describe the goods and the typical buyer. For services, identify the deliverable and delivery method.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article

  1. Replace broad labels with a transaction
  2. Separate multiple activities
  3. Keep descriptions consistent
  4. A formation preparation record
  5. Frequently asked questions

Replace broad labels with a transaction

Instead of international trade, describe the goods and the typical buyer. For services, identify the deliverable and delivery method.

Separate multiple activities

If the business has several revenue streams, describe each and identify the main one. Do not combine unrelated activities into a vague phrase that obscures the model.

Keep descriptions consistent

Use the same accurate explanation in the company brief, website and provider conversations. Explain genuine changes rather than allowing several incompatible versions to circulate.

A formation preparation record

Proposed details
Activity, intended name and company type.
Participants
Proposed owners, directors and their roles.
Local arrangements
Required company functions and correspondence.
Stage
Draft, ready for review, submitted or registered.

Keep proposals separate from the information actually submitted. If a detail changes, confirm which parts of the file need updating and preserve the earlier version where relevant. Use current official forms for the actual company type. This preparation record is not an incorporation form and does not replace the company’s issued documents after successful registration.

Frequently asked questions

Does company formation include automatic account approval?

No. A business account or payment service is a separate provider decision.

Should I use this as the official application form?

No. Use current official forms for the relevant company type and accurate project information.
