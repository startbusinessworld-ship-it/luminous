Company changes: do not wait for the annual return

Changes to a registered-office address or relevant director particulars generally require separate notifications to the Companies Registry within the applicable periods.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 18-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Changes to a registered-office address or relevant director particulars generally require separate notifications to the Companies Registry within the applicable periods. These are not changes to leave unreported until the next annual return. Check the particular detail, prescribed form and filing deadline.

Keep the distinction clear

Use the company’s own documents and official notices to identify obligations and dates. Company filings, tax reporting and renewals are different processes; retain evidence of the action taken for each. Hong Kong Companies Registry: annual returns.

A practical example

If the company changes its registered office, update the formal company record through the relevant process. Changing a website footer or telling a service provider informally is not a substitute for a required notification.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Identify exactly which particulars changed.
  2. Check the correct notice and filing period.
  3. Retain the updated record and submission confirmation.

Can I report every change in the next annual return instead?

No. Some changes have their own notification requirements. Check the Companies Registry guidance for the specific change rather than waiting.
