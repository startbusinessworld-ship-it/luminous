Why consider a Hong Kong company?

  • A legal entity for your activity: organise customer contracts, supplier relationships and company records around your business.
  • A remote starting point: non-Hong Kong residents can incorporate a local limited company and act as directors.
  • Local administration: arrange the Hong Kong registered office and eligible company secretary required for the company.

Choose the structure because it fits where and how you operate. Incorporation alone does not establish a tax exemption or give automatic access to banking. Read the Companies Registry requirements.

What you need before applying

For a private company limited by shares, identify the shareholders, at least one individual director, the shareholding arrangements, the registered office and the company secretary. A sole director cannot also be the company secretary. The secretary must meet Hong Kong eligibility requirements. Check director and secretary rules.

For the Luminos application, prepare three proposed company names, a clear description of the activity and the requested identity and address documents. More information may be needed depending on the ownership structure and business.

How to create your Hong Kong company with Luminos

  1. Create your client account. Open Luminos registration, enter your contact details and follow the account instructions. A portal account starts the application journey; it is not itself an incorporated company.
  2. Prepare the company file. In My files, enter your company-name preferences and business activity. Explain the products or services, customer countries and supplier relationships.
  3. Upload the requested documents. The portal currently requests a passport photo, a selfie holding the passport and proof of address. Use clear files and confirm the requirements for each person involved.
  4. Review the scope with Luminos. Confirm ownership details, services, fees, registered-office and company-secretary arrangements. Respond to any clarification requests.
  5. Check and sign the documents. Review the company particulars and complete the required signatures or consents before the incorporation application is filed.
  6. Organise your next steps. Keep the official company documents when issued, prepare any business-account application and plan ongoing bookkeeping, filings and renewals.

Company formation and business accounts are separate

Your incorporated company can apply for a business account or payment services appropriate to its activity. The bank or provider reviews the application and decides approval, available features and any interview or travel requirements. If you sell online or trade internationally, describe your expected currencies, customers, suppliers and payment flows when discussing the application.

Frequently asked questions

Can I create a Hong Kong company without travelling?

A non-resident can begin and complete the incorporation process remotely with the required local arrangements. Banking is a separate process and may have different requirements.

How long does the process take?

Ask Luminos for the current estimate after your file has been reviewed. Missing information, signatures or further checks can change the schedule. Company incorporation and account opening have different timelines.

Does registering a company mean I pay no tax?

No. Tax treatment depends on the business, the source of profits and the applicable rules, including any obligations where you live or manage the activity. The Inland Revenue Department explains the territorial source principle.

What happens after incorporation?

Retain the company records and organise the relevant filings, renewals and accounting responsibilities. Agree the continuing support scope with the team.
