Why a company certificate cannot guarantee account approval

It confirms company incorporation. It does not explain all the owners, commercial relationships or intended use of a financial service.

Understand what the certificate proves

It confirms company incorporation. It does not explain all the owners, commercial relationships or intended use of a financial service.

Understand the provider's role

Each provider assesses the application under its own process. Requests and outcomes can differ between business profiles and services.

Prepare without promising the outcome

Provide genuine coherent information and answer follow-up questions. Preparation support can improve organisation, but it cannot replace the provider's decision.

An application preparation worksheet

Business model
Goods or services, customers and sales channels.
Ownership
Participants and any ownership chain.
Payments
Expected counterparties, countries and currencies.
Evidence
Genuine records supporting the description.

Use the worksheet to organise a conversation, then follow the selected provider’s current requirements. It is not a universal document list. A new venture should identify forecasts and pre-launch evidence honestly. An existing business should explain its actual activity and any changes. Completing this preparation does not guarantee acceptance, a particular review time or access to every product.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official provider checklist?

No. It is an editorial preparation guide. Follow the current requirements of the provider and product you actually apply for.

Does preparation guarantee approval?

No. The provider decides on the application and can request further information.
