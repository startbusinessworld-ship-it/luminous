Turn your business idea into a Hong Kong company plan

Before filing an incorporation application, turn the idea into a specific business description.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 14-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Before filing an incorporation application, turn the idea into a specific business description. Identify the proposed activity, company name, ownership and director arrangements. This is also the point to discuss whether your work needs additional permissions or services beyond incorporation.

Keep the distinction clear

Company setup and later operations are distinct. Confirm the registered-office and secretary arrangements, then plan the records and obligations that continue after incorporation. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

Selling a physical product, providing a consulting service and operating a regulated activity can require different preparations. A clear description prevents the company paperwork from becoming detached from the business it is meant to support.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Describe the activity and intended customers.
  2. Confirm the proposed name and ownership.
  3. Discuss the registered office, secretary and later obligations.

Should the company name be the first and only decision?

No. The name matters, but the company's activity, people, local requirements and ongoing administration also need to fit the plan.
