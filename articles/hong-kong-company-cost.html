How much does a Hong Kong company cost with Luminos?

Published 19 October 2026Luminos teamLire en français

Luminos offers Hong Kong company formation for USD 1,490. Renewal and annual support cost USD 990 per year. Here is what each package includes and how to prepare your project.

Confirm the scope before ordering

Ask Luminos for written confirmation of government fees and company secretary arrangements within your quote. The published package price should not be interpreted as covering every possible company expense. Accounting, audit, specific licences, tax advice and banking fees must be checked separately.

Formation: a one-time USD 1,490 payment

This package supports incorporation and the first steps of your international business.

  • Company registration.
  • Certificate of Incorporation and Articles of Association.
  • Registered address for one year.
  • Business account application support.
  • Client portal and document access.
  • Dedicated WhatsApp support.

Explain your activity, and we identify the documents to prepare. Your client portal gives you a place to access company records and follow the application.

Renewal: USD 990 per year

Our annual package supports the next stage after incorporation.

  • Annual return filing.
  • Business registration renewal.
  • Registered address renewal.
  • Document updates.
  • Progress tracking in the client portal.
  • Dedicated WhatsApp support.

The packages cover different stages: establishing the company, followed by renewal and annual support.

Define additional needs before ordering

Accounting, audit, a particular licence or specific tax advice require a defined scope. Their cost depends on your file. Account operating charges depend on the institution you choose.

Tell us about your activity, approximate transaction volume, customer and supplier countries, and required currencies. That helps us discuss the support your business actually needs.

Is banking assistance included?

Yes. Support with a business account application is included in formation. The institution retains the approval decision. Onboarding conditions, interviews and available services depend on your profile and provider.

How to get started

Send us a short description of your business and goals. We clarify scope, documents and next steps with you. You can also read our formation guide and business account guide.

The services and prices described reflect the Luminos packages displayed on the publication date.
