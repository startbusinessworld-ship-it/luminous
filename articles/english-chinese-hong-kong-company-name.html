English or Chinese: choosing a Hong Kong company name

A Hong Kong company can register an English name, a Chinese name, or one of each.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 14-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

A Hong Kong company can register an English name, a Chinese name, or one of each. A single registered name cannot combine English words with Chinese characters. Decide the format before working on a final application, then check the applicable name rules.

Keep the distinction clear

Company registration and brand protection serve different purposes. A proposed name should be checked under the company-name rules, with trade mark questions considered separately. Hong Kong Companies Registry: company names.

A practical example

A founder serving English-speaking customers may choose an English registered name. Another business may register both language versions. Neither choice replaces a separate trade mark review for the brand being used.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Choose an English name, Chinese name, or both.
  2. Check each proposed name under the applicable rules.
  3. Keep the registered names consistent in company records.

Can I mix English and Chinese in one registered name?

The Companies Registry does not permit a single company name that combines English words and Chinese characters. Separate English and Chinese names are possible.
