Your first Hong Kong company discussion: a meeting checklist

A short preparation checklist can make the first conversation much more productive. The aim is to leave with a clear next step rather than an unstructured collection of document requests.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article

  1. Before the meeting
  2. During the discussion

Before the meeting

Prepare the activity description, intended markets and proposed owners. Identify whether you already trade through another entity. Bring questions about formation, account preparation and continuing support separately.

During the discussion

Explain constraints honestly, including location, launch plans and missing documents. Ask which points are confirmed and which require a provider or authority's review. Record assumptions that still need checking.

After the meeting

Create a short action log with the item, responsible person and requested evidence. Confirm how to send sensitive documents securely. Avoid sending the same file in several versions through multiple channels.

Know when the stage is complete

Agree how submission and completion will be communicated. Do not interpret a document request, payment or preliminary discussion as confirmation that an account has been approved.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

What if I do not know all the answers?

List the unknowns clearly; do not fill gaps with estimates presented as facts.

What should the meeting produce?

A clear scope and a practical next action, with any outstanding requirements identified.
