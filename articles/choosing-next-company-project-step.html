What must be resolved before proceeding?

For an early-stage business, complete the worksheet with confirmed facts first. Leave an unknown marked as unknown and assign a way to resolve it. For an existing business, use actual contracts and transactions to test whether the current description is still accurate. The worksheet is a discussion aid, not a determination of legal or tax status.
