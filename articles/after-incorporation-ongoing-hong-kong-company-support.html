After incorporation: organising ongoing Hong Kong company support

Incorporation creates the company, but work continues afterwards. Set up an administration routine before records, correspondence and filing requests begin arriving from different directions.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article

  2. Keep commercial records from day one
  3. Record changes promptly

  5. An administration action log
  6. Frequently asked questions

Allocate the recurring responsibilities

Identify who handles company correspondence, keeps records and monitors requested filings. Ask which services are included and which need separate instructions. Do not assume every future task is covered by the incorporation stage.

Keep commercial records from day one

Store invoices, agreements, statements and relevant supporting evidence. Make sure payments can be matched to transactions. Reconstructing a year of activity from scattered messages is harder than keeping a simple routine.

Record changes promptly

When details or the business model change, tell the relevant adviser or provider and ask what update is needed. Keep the date and supporting record. A change in a marketing profile does not update official company information.

Review the support arrangement

Periodically check that the contact, access permissions and responsibilities still work. Keep a clear route for unusual requests instead of waiting until a deadline makes them urgent.

An administration action log

Task
The correspondence, record or filing question.
Responsible person
Who coordinates it and who supplies inputs.
Dependency
Missing information or an external decision.
Completion evidence
What confirms the task is actually finished.

Use the log alongside the company’s actual requirements and agreed service scope. It does not replace an official filing calendar. Review notices and questions as they arrive and keep proof of responses. If activity or company details change, ask which updates are needed rather than assuming an old checklist continues to cover the new circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

Is ongoing administration the same as account approval?

No. Account services remain subject to the relevant provider's own process.

Does a company with little activity need to keep records?

Do not assume limited activity removes obligations; confirm the applicable position and retain the relevant evidence.
