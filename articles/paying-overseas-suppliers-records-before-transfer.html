Paying overseas suppliers: records to prepare before a transfer

Keep the purchase agreement, quotation or invoice that explains the payment. Check that the recipient relates to the actual supplier arrangement.

Identify the commercial reason

Keep the purchase agreement, quotation or invoice that explains the payment. Check that the recipient relates to the actual supplier arrangement.

Match the payment details carefully

Confirm counterparty information through an appropriate trusted process. Record the currency, purpose and transaction reference. Explain deposits or split payments rather than leaving them ambiguous.

Keep the later completion evidence

Add delivery or other relevant records to the same transaction file. A completed payment is one part of the commercial story, not proof that every part of the trade is complete.

A trade transaction worksheet

Role
Buyer, reseller, agent or other genuine role.
Counterparties
Who supplies, buys, invoices and pays.
Route
Actual movement of goods and actual payment route.
Evidence
Agreement, order, invoice and available delivery records.

Work through one real or clearly labelled planned transaction before describing the entire business. Note any deposits, intermediaries or split shipments. The worksheet helps explain the model but does not establish customs compliance or permission to trade a particular product. Check those requirements for the actual goods and jurisdictions rather than assuming company registration answers them.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a mandatory official checklist?

No. The worksheet is a practical preparation tool. Actual requirements depend on the task, company and relevant recipient.
