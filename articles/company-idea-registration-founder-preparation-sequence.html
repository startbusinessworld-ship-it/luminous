From company idea to registration: a founder's preparation sequence

A clear preparation sequence reduces confusion between proposed details, submitted information and issued records. Use the carousel as an overview, then maintain a file for each stage.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

Confirm the proposal

Write down the activity, intended name, participants and management arrangements. Check which information is still provisional. Do not circulate several conflicting versions as if they were all final.

Prepare the application information

Use the current requirements for the company type and keep supporting documents consistent. Ask about missing or unusual items before submission. A visually complete folder may still contain inaccurate information.

Record the submission and result

Keep a copy of the submitted file and later official records. Label the stage clearly. If further information is requested, attach the response to the relevant request rather than losing it in a general message thread.

Start the ongoing working file

Organise certificates, company records and commercial evidence. Prepare separate account information where needed. The end of incorporation is the beginning of an administration routine.

A formation preparation record

Proposed details
Activity, intended name and company type.
Participants
Proposed owners, directors and their roles.
Local arrangements
Required company functions and correspondence.
Stage
Draft, ready for review, submitted or registered.

Keep proposals separate from the information actually submitted. If a detail changes, confirm which parts of the file need updating and preserve the earlier version where relevant. Use current official forms for the actual company type. This preparation record is not an incorporation form and does not replace the company’s issued documents after successful registration.

Frequently asked questions

What if the plan changes during preparation?

Explain the change and confirm which documents need updating before proceeding.

Is the same folder suitable for every provider?

No. Use the actual recipient's checklist and share only what is needed.
