Can one person be shareholder and director?

A sole shareholder can also be a director of a Hong Kong private company.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 19-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

A sole shareholder can also be a director of a Hong Kong private company. Ownership and management are different roles even when one person holds both. Where there is only one director, that person cannot also act as company secretary.

Keep the distinction clear

Ownership, management and company-secretarial responsibilities have different functions. Record the appointments accurately and check the relevant eligibility rules before filing. Hong Kong Companies Registry: directors and company secretary.

A practical example

A solo founder can hold the shares and manage the company as director while appointing an eligible secretary separately. Keeping the roles clear helps complete the company particulars correctly.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Identify who owns the shares.
  2. Record the director appointment separately.
  3. Arrange an eligible company secretary.

Does having one shareholder remove the secretary requirement?

No. A solo ownership arrangement does not remove the company's company-secretary requirement or the restriction applying to a sole director.
