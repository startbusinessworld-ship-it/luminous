Do you need to move after Hong Kong incorporation?

There is no Hong Kong-residence requirement for a company director under the incorporation rules discussed here.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 18-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

There is no Hong Kong-residence requirement for a company director under the incorporation rules discussed here. The company still needs its local registered office and eligible secretary. Separately consider the rules that apply where you actually live, work and manage the business.

Keep the distinction clear

Remote incorporation does not remove local company requirements. A registered office in Hong Kong and an eligible company secretary remain part of the setup, while banking and personal obligations require separate consideration. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

An overseas founder may remain abroad while using a local registered-office and secretarial arrangement. That setup should not be described as automatically resolving every tax or reporting obligation in the founder's home country.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Separate your personal residence from the company address.
  2. Keep local company requirements in place.
  3. Review obligations in the places where the business operates.

Does a Hong Kong company decide my personal tax residence?

No. Company incorporation and personal tax residence are different matters. Your circumstances and the relevant jurisdictions' rules need separate consideration.
