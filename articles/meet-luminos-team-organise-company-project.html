Meet the Luminos team: how to organise your company project

The team presentation introduces the people behind the conversation. To make that support useful, organise the project around clear information, responsibilities and follow-up.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article

  1. Begin with the business, not the paperwork
  2. Create one shared project brief
  3. Understand the service boundaries

Begin with the business, not the paperwork

Explain the activity and what you want the company to do. Documents should support that explanation. A folder of identification and certificates cannot by itself describe the commercial model.

Create one shared project brief

Agree the proposed participants, countries and next objective. Keep a dated version that the people involved can refer to. Update it when the plan changes so different conversations do not use conflicting assumptions.

Understand the service boundaries

Company formation support, account application preparation and ongoing administration have different outputs. External authorities and providers retain their own decisions. Ask the team to distinguish those stages clearly.

Use a structured follow-up

Keep a list of requests, evidence provided and remaining questions. Confirm the appropriate secure sharing method. A short organised exchange helps the team respond to the actual issue.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

What should an introduction to the team include?

Explain the activity, current company stage and the specific next task, rather than sending an unexplained collection of documents.
