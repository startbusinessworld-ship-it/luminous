Choose an operational result

Examples include completing a company document index or clarifying the supplier payment workflow. A result should describe something you can check, not only an ambition.

Identify the missing inputs

List the people, information and third-party responses needed. Distinguish tasks you control from decisions made by a provider or authority.

Review progress against evidence

Keep the completed documents or agreed process beside the objective. Revise the plan when real activity changes rather than treating the original projection as a fact.

What must be resolved before proceeding?

For an early-stage business, complete the worksheet with confirmed facts first. Leave an unknown marked as unknown and assign a way to resolve it. For an existing business, use actual contracts and transactions to test whether the current description is still accurate. The worksheet is a discussion aid, not a determination of legal or tax status.
