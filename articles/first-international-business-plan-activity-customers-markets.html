Separate buyers from markets

Describe the customer type, not just a country list. A retailer selling consumer products and a consultant serving companies need different operating explanations.

Identify the next unanswered question

Choose whether the immediate task is evaluating a company structure, preparing documents or understanding payment needs. Keep other ambitions in the plan without presenting them as completed arrangements.

What must be resolved before proceeding?

For an early-stage business, complete the worksheet with confirmed facts first. Leave an unknown marked as unknown and assign a way to resolve it. For an existing business, use actual contracts and transactions to test whether the current description is still accurate. The worksheet is a discussion aid, not a determination of legal or tax status.
