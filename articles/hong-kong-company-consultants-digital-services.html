Hong Kong companies for consultants and digital services

A service business should be able to explain what it delivers, how it contracts and where its work is performed.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 16-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

A service business should be able to explain what it delivers, how it contracts and where its work is performed. A company provides an entity for those relationships, but it does not make all overseas service income automatically offshore or settle the founder's personal position elsewhere.

Keep the distinction clear

A company structure, payment arrangements and the rules in the countries where a business operates are separate considerations. Plan them together without assuming that incorporation settles every cross-border question. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

A designer serving clients abroad can retain project agreements, evidence of delivery and payment records. Those documents help explain the activity when preparing accounts or an application to a financial institution.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Describe the deliverable and client relationship.
  2. Keep contracts, invoices and delivery evidence connected.
  3. Consider rules where services are performed and managed.

Does selling a digital service remove record-keeping requirements?

No. Service businesses still need appropriate records supporting their transactions, income and expenditure under the applicable rules.
