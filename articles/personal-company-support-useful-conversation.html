Personal company support: preparing a useful conversation with the team

A real conversation is most useful when it connects your question to the business behind it. Explain what you are building and what is currently preventing the next step.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article
  1. Begin with the present position
  2. Make the request specific
  3. Explain the available evidence
  4. End with a written action
  5. A useful follow-up record
  6. Frequently asked questions

Begin with the present position

Say whether the company exists, whether trading has started and whether an account application is already under review. Include any previous requests you are trying to answer. This avoids repeating work based on an outdated picture.

Make the request specific

Ask for help with a defined task such as organising the company file or understanding the next preparation step. If several tasks are involved, put them in priority order and identify dependencies.

Explain the available evidence

List the documents you have and the ones that are missing. Do not send sensitive records just to fill a conversation. Confirm the secure method and the reason a document is needed before sharing it.

End with a written action

Record who will do what next and what information is expected. If an outcome depends on an authority or provider, mark that dependency. A practical follow-up builds confidence through clarity.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

What if I already have a company?

Explain the existing company and the specific support needed; formation may not be the relevant first step.

Will every question be resolved in one conversation?

Not necessarily. Some matters require document review or an external decision.
