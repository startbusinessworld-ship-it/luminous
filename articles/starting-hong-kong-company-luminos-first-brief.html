Starting a Hong Kong company with Luminos: preparing your first brief

The fastest useful first conversation starts with a clear description of your business. Prepare a short brief so that formation, banking preparation and continuing support can be discussed separately.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

Describe the work in plain English

State what you sell, who buys it and how you deliver it. Include whether the business has started or is still planned. Avoid broad labels such as international business without an example transaction.

List the people and locations

Identify proposed owners and directors, where they live and where the business will be managed. Explain the role of any existing company involved. Do not send sensitive identification through an unverified channel.

Bring the open questions

Make a short list about documents, local company functions, account applications and later administration. Mark what is urgent and what can wait. This makes the follow-up more useful than an open-ended request to do everything.

Agree the next deliverable

Ask what information is needed next, how to provide it securely and what completion means for that stage. A company application and an approved business account should never be treated as the same deliverable.

A formation preparation record

Proposed details
Activity, intended name and company type.
Participants
Proposed owners, directors and their roles.
Local arrangements
Required company functions and correspondence.
Stage
Draft, ready for review, submitted or registered.

Keep proposals separate from the information actually submitted. If a detail changes, confirm which parts of the file need updating and preserve the earlier version where relevant. Use current official forms for the actual company type. This preparation record is not an incorporation form and does not replace the company’s issued documents after successful registration.
