How to create a Hong Kong company with Luminos: step-by-step

Discover the advantages of Hong Kong, then follow the Luminos tutorial: create your account, prepare your documents and submit your company file.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 19-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

Why choose Hong Kong? The short version

A Hong Kong company can give your international business a legal entity for contracts, supplier relationships and business-account applications. Non-residents can incorporate a local limited company; you do not have to relocate to become its director. A Hong Kong registered office and an eligible company secretary are still required.

Hong Kong uses a territorial approach to profits tax. Offshore treatment depends on the operations that generate the profits and the applicable rules; overseas clients do not automatically mean zero tax. Your obligations where you live or manage the business also need separate consideration.

What this tutorial covers

This walkthrough follows the Luminos website and client portal. Creating a portal account starts your application journey; it does not itself incorporate a company or open a business account.

Step 1 — Create your Luminos account

Open the Luminos registration page and choose Create account. Enter your first and last name, email address, WhatsApp number and a password of at least eight characters. Click Create my account and follow any verification instructions. Already registered? Choose Already have an account to sign in.

Step 2 — Prepare three company names

In your client portal, open My files. Under Desired company names, enter three choices in order of preference. Luminos can review the proposed names and check availability; a name is not reserved just because you enter it.

Step 3 — Explain what your company will do

Complete Business activity in plain English. Describe what you sell, your customer and supplier countries, and how you expect to receive and send payments.

Example: “We sell home accessories through our online store to customers in Europe and source products from suppliers in Asia.” Include the real activity rather than a broad label such as “international business”.

Step 4 — Upload the requested documents

The current My files screen requests:

  • A clear passport photo, with the full document and readable text.
  • A selfie holding the open passport, with your face and the photo page visible.
  • Proof of address, such as a utility bill or bank statement, subject to the team’s acceptance requirements.

Upload these through your signed-in portal. Confirm the requirements for every shareholder, director and beneficial owner with the team; additional information may be requested depending on the ownership structure and activity.

Step 5 — Submit your file for review

Check the names, activity and uploads, then click Submit my file. Respond to any clarification requests. Confirm your service scope, fees, ownership structure, registered-office and company-secretary arrangements with Luminos before incorporation proceeds.

Step 6 — Review and sign the incorporation documents

Luminos prepares the application within the agreed service scope. Review the company particulars and complete the required signatures or consents. The application is submitted to the Companies Registry. Incorporation is complete when the authorities issue the relevant certificates, not when you create your portal account.

The estimated schedule should be confirmed for your complete file; missing information or additional checks can change it.

Step 7 — Plan your business account and ongoing administration

After incorporation, keep your Certificate of Incorporation, Business Registration Certificate and company records organised. Discuss your banking or payment needs with Luminos and prepare evidence of your business. A bank or payment provider conducts its own review and decides approval. Arrange bookkeeping, filings and renewals separately within the agreed support plan.
