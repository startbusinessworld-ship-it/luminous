Starting a Hong Kong company: your first conversation

Start with the business you actually want to run, rather than choosing a company structure from a slogan.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 19-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Start with the business you actually want to run, rather than choosing a company structure from a slogan. Explain what you sell, where your customers are, who your suppliers are and where you make decisions. These details help separate incorporation, payment access and ongoing administration into a realistic plan.

Keep the distinction clear

Company setup and later operations are distinct. Confirm the registered-office and secretary arrangements, then plan the records and obligations that continue after incorporation. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

A consultant serving overseas clients and a trader buying physical goods may both consider a Hong Kong company. Their contracts, evidence of activity and payment needs will differ, even if the incorporation form looks similar.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Describe your product or service in plain language.
  2. List the people who will own and manage the company.
  3. Separate company formation from banking and annual support.

Do I need to understand every filing before contacting Luminos?

No. Bring a clear description of your business and the team can explain the relevant formation steps and what remains outside the service scope.
