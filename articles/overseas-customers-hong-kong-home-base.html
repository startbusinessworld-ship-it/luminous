A Hong Kong home base for overseas customer sales

Give cross-border sales a consistent company identity.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 18-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Give cross-border sales a consistent company identity. The same entity should be clear across storefront information, commercial documents and payment applications. A Hong Kong registration can establish that entity, but it does not replace the records explaining how it actually trades.

Keep the distinction clear

The company and storefront should describe a consistent business. Payment-product access, record keeping and rules relevant to the markets where you operate need separate attention. Hong Kong Monetary Authority: information for account opening.

A practical example

A growing store can review whether its customer invoices, supplier purchases and payment records identify the same company. Inconsistency creates questions that a company certificate alone cannot answer.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Check which entity is selling to customers.
  2. Align commercial documents with that entity.
  3. Retain evidence of the transactions and relationships.

Can my store brand differ from the registered company name?

Branding and registered names serve different purposes. Make the contracting entity clear, and check separate trade mark and other applicable requirements.
