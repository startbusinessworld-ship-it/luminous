Separate director residence from company requirements

A Hong Kong director can live abroad. The company still needs the required registered office and eligible company secretary arrangements in Hong Kong.

Make remote decisions workable

Agree access to records, correspondence and approval requests. Ensure that notices do not sit unread because the director is in another country.

Consider the wider operating circumstances

Describe where management and work actually occur when seeking advice. A non-resident director arrangement does not resolve every obligation in another jurisdiction.

A remote company coordination plan

Correspondence
Who receives notices and forwards them.
Decisions
Who reviews and approves the next action.
Records
Where current company documents are stored.
Continuity
How an appropriate alternative contact gets access.

Test the plan before a time-sensitive request arrives. Confirm that the director can see the relevant documents and that messages reach the responsible contact. Keep secure access arrangements separate from ordinary chat messages. Remote coordination does not remove local company requirements or determine obligations connected with where people actually work and manage the business.

Frequently asked questions

Does company formation include automatic account approval?

No. A business account or payment service is a separate provider decision.
