Annual return or tax return: which filing is which?

An annual return records company particulars with the Companies Registry.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 19-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

An annual return records company particulars with the Companies Registry. A profits tax return concerns the company's tax reporting to the Inland Revenue Department. Submitting one does not complete the other. Build separate reminders and keep the relevant records for each process.

Keep the distinction clear

Use the company’s own documents and official notices to identify obligations and dates. Company filings, tax reporting and renewals are different processes; retain evidence of the action taken for each. Hong Kong Companies Registry: annual returns.

A practical example

A founder who files an annual return should not assume that an outstanding tax notice has been answered. Check the addressee, issuing authority and required action on each document.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Identify the authority issuing each notice.
  2. Track the annual return and tax deadlines separately.
  3. Keep confirmation of each submission.

Does an annual return report my company's taxable profit?

No. The annual return concerns company particulars. Tax reporting is a separate process with the Inland Revenue Department.
