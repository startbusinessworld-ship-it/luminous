Choosing a business base for 2027: a Hong Kong decision checklist

A country ranking cannot decide where your company should operate. The useful question is whether a particular structure fits your customers, management, suppliers and reporting responsibilities.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

Replace rankings with a business map

Write down where decisions are made, where goods or services are delivered and where the owners live. These are different questions. A Hong Kong company does not move your personal residence or automatically remove obligations elsewhere.

Compare the operating requirements

Look at administration, the address and company secretary arrangements, access to suitable payment services and the evidence your business can provide. Compare the ongoing work as carefully as incorporation itself.

Read the original visual critically

The social image is an editorial comparison, not an official league table or a prediction of future tax rules. Use it to start a discussion rather than to justify a structure. Ask for current requirements that apply to your particular activity.

Build a decision record

Keep a short note of the alternatives considered, the operational reason for Hong Kong and the questions still requiring advice. Revisit that note if the business model or management location changes.

What must be resolved before proceeding?

For an early-stage business, complete the worksheet with confirmed facts first. Leave an unknown marked as unknown and assign a way to resolve it. For an existing business, use actual contracts and transactions to test whether the current description is still accurate. The worksheet is a discussion aid, not a determination of legal or tax status.

Frequently asked questions

How should I compare jurisdictions?

Use your actual operating model and obtain advice on the jurisdictions involved; a general ranking is insufficient.

Does incorporating in Hong Kong change my personal tax residence?

No. Company incorporation and personal residence are separate matters that require their own analysis.
