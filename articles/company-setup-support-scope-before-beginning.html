What company setup support should cover before you begin

Before beginning, understand the scope of support rather than relying on a short service label. A defined scope helps both the entrepreneur and the team know what is expected.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article
  1. Ask for the deliverables
  2. Identify your own inputs
  3. Clarify external dependencies
  4. Plan the handover after formation
  5. A useful follow-up record
  6. Frequently asked questions

Ask for the deliverables

Separate preparation, filing coordination, local company functions and continuing administration. Ask what evidence confirms completion at each stage. Keep the agreed scope with your project records.

Identify your own inputs

The company must provide accurate participant and activity information. Assign who collects it and who reviews it. Support cannot replace knowledge of your own business or make unsupported information reliable.

Clarify external dependencies

An authority or provider can request more information or make its own decision. Ask how those requests are handled. Be cautious of treating an estimated timetable as a guaranteed approval date.

Plan the handover after formation

Know how to access issued documents and who handles later correspondence. Discuss ongoing tasks before the initial project closes so responsibilities remain clear.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

Should account preparation be listed separately?

Yes. It is distinct from incorporation and the provider decides on the application.

What makes a scope useful?

Specific outputs, required inputs and responsibilities rather than a general promise to handle everything.
