Director residence and your Hong Kong company address

A director does not have to be a Hong Kong resident.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 16-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

A director does not have to be a Hong Kong resident. That does not remove the company's local requirements: a registered office must be situated in Hong Kong, and the company secretary must satisfy the relevant local rules. Personal residence and company address serve different purposes.

Keep the distinction clear

Remote incorporation does not remove local company requirements. A registered office in Hong Kong and an eligible company secretary remain part of the setup, while banking and personal obligations require separate consideration. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

A founder living overseas can remain the director while the company maintains a registered office in Hong Kong. The director's overseas home should not be treated as a replacement for that local registered office.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Record the director's details accurately.
  2. Arrange a compliant Hong Kong registered office.
  3. Confirm who will receive and act on official correspondence.

Can my overseas home be the company's Hong Kong registered office?

No. The registered office of the Hong Kong company must be situated in Hong Kong, even where its director lives elsewhere.
