Brand-name checks before Hong Kong incorporation

Check your name before building the launch around it.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 16-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Check your name before building the launch around it. Company-name registration and trade-mark registration are separate systems, so a company-name check should not be treated as complete brand clearance. Plan both checks before putting the proposed name on packaging, storefronts and customer contracts.

Keep the distinction clear

Company registration and brand protection serve different purposes. A proposed name should be checked under the company-name rules, with trade mark questions considered separately. Hong Kong Companies Registry: company names.

A practical example

A merchant preparing a new product line can shortlist names and review registration issues before printing stock. If a concern arises, revising the shortlist early is more practical than changing a launched brand.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Shortlist company and brand names.
  2. Check company-name and trade-mark records separately.
  3. Resolve uncertainty before committing to branded materials.

Should I wait until after incorporation to think about trade marks?

The company and brand decisions are connected commercially even though registration systems differ. Checking early can reveal issues before you commit to a name.
