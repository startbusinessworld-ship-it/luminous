Hong Kong company documents explained: CI, BR, NNC1 and Articles

A company file contains documents with different jobs. Understanding those jobs helps you answer requests accurately instead of sending a folder of unrelated paperwork.

Certificate of Incorporation: the formation record

The Certificate of Incorporation confirms that the company has been incorporated. Check the company name and identifying information against the rest of your file. It does not itself establish that a provider has approved an account or a regulated activity.

Business Registration Certificate: a separate purpose

The BR relates to business registration. Keep the current certificate and track its validity. Do not describe it as permission for every business activity; specific activities can require additional licences or approvals.

Application records and company rules

For a company limited by shares, NNC1 records incorporation information and the Articles of Association set out the company's rules. IRBR1 forms part of the one-stop incorporation and business registration application. Keep the submitted versions and issued records distinct.

Build an indexed company folder

Separate issued certificates, application copies, ownership and director records, and later changes. Use dated filenames and restrict access to personal information. The carousel contains educational adaptations with client details redacted, not blank official forms for reuse.

A document index you can maintain

Type
Name the record and its purpose.
Version
Record the date and distinguish current from historical.
Location
Identify the secure source file or physical record.
Access
Record who needs the document for which task.

Check the recipient’s request before sharing a bundle. A redacted public illustration, an application draft and an issued certificate should never be treated as interchangeable. Keep the full working record securely and share the minimum relevant information. If a record appears inconsistent, ask how to correct the underlying information rather than editing an official document to make it look right.

Frequently asked questions

Are the CI and BR interchangeable?

No. They serve different purposes and a request may require both.

Can I use the illustrated documents to submit an application?

No. Use the current official forms and your own accurate information; these visuals explain the documents only.
