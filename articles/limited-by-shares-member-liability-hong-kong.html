What limited by shares means in Hong Kong

For a company limited by shares, members' liability as members is limited to any amount unpaid on their shares.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 19-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

For a company limited by shares, members' liability as members is limited to any amount unpaid on their shares. This describes a feature of the company structure. It does not mean that the company has no debts, filing obligations or tax responsibilities, or that every personal undertaking is automatically protected.

Keep the distinction clear

The company structure sets out legal relationships and responsibilities. It is not a substitute for planning operations, funding and the obligations that follow incorporation. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

A founder comparing structures should distinguish the company's obligations from the member's position in relation to shares. Any separate guarantee or personal commitment should be assessed on its own terms.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Understand the shareholding arrangement.
  2. Separate company obligations from personal commitments.
  3. Review the structure before signing additional undertakings.

Does limited liability remove the company's administrative obligations?

No. The company still has obligations under the applicable company, tax and other rules. Limited liability is not an exemption from compliance.
