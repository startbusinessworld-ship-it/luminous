Hong Kong business accounts in 2026: documents and application steps

Updated 19 October 2026Luminos teamLire en français

Incorporating in Hong Kong does not automatically provide a business account. In 2026, each institution assesses applications against its products, supported countries and risk policies.

What documents should you prepare?

Prepare company registration records, ownership details and information on directors, beneficial owners and authorised persons. The institution may request business evidence and the source of funds. Its own checklist applies; no single application pack works for every bank. [1]

Remote or in person?

Confirm the process for your profile before booking travel or paying a provider. Starting an application online does not establish that every verification step can be completed remotely. Interviews or further checks may be required. [1]

Compare the actual service

Review supported currencies and countries, exchange charges, transfer fees, cards, limits and how funds are protected. Payment providers and banks do not necessarily offer the same services. Select a solution for your expected transactions, rather than for its name alone.

Prepare a practical business summary

  • Describe your activity, customers and suppliers clearly.
  • Gather available contracts, invoices, website information and evidence of expected transactions.
  • List anticipated countries, currencies and transaction volumes.
  • Keep answers and supporting documents consistent throughout the application.

What Luminos does

We help you prepare and follow your application. The institution makes the final decision; approval and a universal processing time cannot be guaranteed. Account charges are separate from the company formation price. Review both the formation scope and the institution’s terms before ordering.
