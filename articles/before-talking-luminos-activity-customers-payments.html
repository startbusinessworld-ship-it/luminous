Before talking to Luminos: activity, customers and payment questions

A useful discussion begins with the commercial facts. Bring a simple description of the activity, intended customers and payment pattern so the team can distinguish the support tasks.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article
  1. Explain the activity in one concrete example
  2. Describe customers and suppliers
  3. Outline the money movement
  4. Bring a question and a next-step objective
  5. A useful follow-up record
  6. Frequently asked questions

Explain the activity in one concrete example

Describe what a customer buys and how the business delivers it. If there are several activities, identify the main one and any planned additions. Avoid a vague description that could mean unrelated businesses.

Describe customers and suppliers

State the types of counterparties and the markets involved. Explain existing relationships and planned ones separately. Keep commercially sensitive details for the appropriate secure channel.

Outline the money movement

Identify how customers pay and how suppliers or service providers are paid. Include currencies and expected frequency. Use realistic estimates and make their status clear.

Bring a question and a next-step objective

Say whether you need company formation, help organising an account application or administration support. Leave with an action list rather than assuming every topic has been completed.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to share private documents in the first message?

Start with the business description; confirm the secure document route when information is requested.

What if my model is still changing?

Explain what is confirmed and what is undecided, then update the brief when decisions are made.
