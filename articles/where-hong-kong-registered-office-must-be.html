Where must a Hong Kong registered office be?

The registered office must be situated in Hong Kong.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 18-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

The registered office must be situated in Hong Kong. It is the company's official address for relevant communications and records, rather than a statement about where the founder lives. When considering an address service, clarify the service scope and how official correspondence will reach you.

Keep the distinction clear

Remote incorporation does not remove local company requirements. A registered office in Hong Kong and an eligible company secretary remain part of the setup, while banking and personal obligations require separate consideration. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

A founder managing the business from another country can use an appropriate local registered-office arrangement. The important operational question is who monitors communications and passes on matters requiring action.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Confirm that the address satisfies the local requirement.
  2. Clarify mail receipt and forwarding arrangements.
  3. Keep the company address details current.

Does a registered office mean I must personally rent a working desk?

Personal workspace and the company's registered-office requirement are different matters. Check that the arrangement offered actually satisfies the company's requirements.
