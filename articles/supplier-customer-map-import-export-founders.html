Connect the map to evidence

Keep genuine quotations, orders or contracts behind the diagram. Mark planned relationships as planned and update the map when operations change.

A trade transaction worksheet

Role
Buyer, reseller, agent or other genuine role.
Counterparties
Who supplies, buys, invoices and pays.
Route
Actual movement of goods and actual payment route.
Evidence
Agreement, order, invoice and available delivery records.

Work through one real or clearly labelled planned transaction before describing the entire business. Note any deposits, intermediaries or split shipments. The worksheet helps explain the model but does not establish customs compliance or permission to trade a particular product. Check those requirements for the actual goods and jurisdictions rather than assuming company registration answers them.
