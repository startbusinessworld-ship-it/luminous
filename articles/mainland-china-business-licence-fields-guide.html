Read the identity fields together

The registered company name and unified social credit code help identify the enterprise. Match them to the records you are reviewing rather than relying on a translated marketing name alone.

Look at address and business scope

These fields describe the registered entity and activity. They do not answer every question about premises, regulated operations or permissions needed for a particular transaction.

Use examples only as examples

The story uses an educational specimen, not an issued client licence. For an actual application, check current instructions for the proposed company, investor and registration location.

A China registration preparation map

Location
The competent local registration authority.
Investors
Investor type and identity or qualification records.
Activity
Proposed business scope and conditional approvals.
Premises
The evidence relevant to the actual registered premises.

Confirm the current local instructions before collecting documents. The Beijing guide provides an official example of the main categories, not a guarantee of identical requirements throughout mainland China. Investor circumstances, activity and location matter. Keep application records distinct from the resulting business licence and from separate account or operational arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

Does this describe every city’s process?

No. Check current local requirements for the proposed location, investor and activity.

Is a mainland licence the same as a Hong Kong BR?

No. Mainland China and Hong Kong use different company registration systems.
