Identify the BR's role

The Business Registration Certificate concerns business registration. Keep it distinct from the Certificate of Incorporation, which confirms company incorporation.

Check the relevant period and details

Use the current certificate requested for the particular task. Keep older versions in the historical file without presenting them as current.

Do not treat it as universal permission

A BR is not an activity-specific licence or account approval. Check any separate requirements for the actual business. The public visual is an educational adaptation with identifying details masked.

A document index you can maintain

Type
Name the record and its purpose.
Version
Record the date and distinguish current from historical.
Location
Identify the secure source file or physical record.
Access
Record who needs the document for which task.

Check the recipient’s request before sharing a bundle. A redacted public illustration, an application draft and an issued certificate should never be treated as interchangeable. Keep the full working record securely and share the minimum relevant information. If a record appears inconsistent, ask how to correct the underlying information rather than editing an official document to make it look right.

Frequently asked questions

Can the pictured document be used for an application?

No. The public visual explains the document and may be redacted or adapted. Use current official forms and your own genuine records.

Should I share the full file publicly?

No. Review personal and confidential information and use a properly redacted presentation copy where appropriate.
