Three stages of a Hong Kong company project: prepare, register, organise

The three-step carousel is a useful overview, but each stage has a different purpose. Preparation makes the file coherent, registration creates the company and organisation keeps the business ready to operate.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article
  1. Stage one: prepare the information
  2. Stage two: submit the company application
  3. Stage three: organise the working file
  4. Put responsibilities beside the stages
  5. A formation preparation record
  6. Frequently asked questions

Stage one: prepare the information

Confirm the proposed activity, name and company participants. Collect the information needed for the selected company type and arrange the local company functions. Resolve inconsistencies before treating the application as ready.

Stage two: submit the company application

For a company limited by shares, the incorporation package includes the relevant incorporation form, Articles of Association and business registration notice. Submission and successful registration are distinct events.

Stage three: organise the working file

Keep issued certificates and company records accessible. Plan correspondence, later filings and accounting records. Prepare any account application as a separate provider process.

Put responsibilities beside the stages

Assign who supplies information, checks it, submits it and follows up. A simple responsibility list prevents the project from stopping because everyone assumed somebody else was handling a request.

A formation preparation record

Proposed details
Activity, intended name and company type.
Participants
Proposed owners, directors and their roles.
Local arrangements
Required company functions and correspondence.
Stage
Draft, ready for review, submitted or registered.

Keep proposals separate from the information actually submitted. If a detail changes, confirm which parts of the file need updating and preserve the earlier version where relevant. Use current official forms for the actual company type. This preparation record is not an incorporation form and does not replace the company’s issued documents after successful registration.

Frequently asked questions

Does registration complete business banking?

No. Account opening is a separate application and review.

Can every project follow the same timeline?

No. Completeness, company details and third-party requirements affect the sequence and timing.
