Calculate the 42-day annual return deadline

A local private company normally delivers its annual return within 42 days after its incorporation anniversary, except in the incorporation year.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 15-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

A local private company normally delivers its annual return within 42 days after its incorporation anniversary, except in the incorporation year. The relevant starting point is the company's own anniversary, not automatically the beginning or end of January.

Keep the distinction clear

Use the company’s own documents and official notices to identify obligations and dates. Company filings, tax reporting and renewals are different processes; retain evidence of the action taken for each. Hong Kong Companies Registry: annual returns.

A practical example

A company incorporated during the year should use that incorporation date to plan its future annual-return reminders. The Companies Registry provides an official calculator, which is more reliable than copying another company's deadline.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Find the incorporation date on the company's documents.
  2. Use the official annual-return guidance and calculator.
  3. Plan preparation early enough for timely delivery.

Is January the annual-return deadline for every company?

No. For the local private company described here, the deadline is linked to its incorporation anniversary, subject to the applicable rules.
