Keep communication practical

Use a short action list, secure document sharing and clear updates when facts change. Confidence comes from traceable work, not from a promise of every approval.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.
