Administration · Practical guide

A new-year operating review for your Hong Kong company

A new year is a useful point to review how the company operates. Focus on the records and responsibilities that will support real transactions, rather than changing the company story for a seasonal campaign.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article
  1. Review what changed
  2. Check the working file
  3. Refresh the responsibility list
  4. Choose a practical objective
  5. An administration action log
  6. Frequently asked questions

Review what changed

List new products, markets, owners, service providers or management arrangements. Separate confirmed changes from plans. Ask whether any official records, provider information or contracts need updating.

Check the working file

Confirm access to certificates, ownership records, contracts and statements. Identify missing periods and unclear transaction references. Deal with gaps while the people and correspondence are still easy to find.

Refresh the responsibility list

Confirm who monitors correspondence and who coordinates administration. Ensure more than one appropriate person knows how to locate essential records. Review permissions when team members change.

Choose a practical objective

Pick a measurable preparation goal such as completing a document index or reconciling outstanding evidence requests. A broad ambition to go global becomes useful when attached to a specific next action.

An administration action log

Task
The correspondence, record or filing question.
Responsible person
Who coordinates it and who supplies inputs.
Dependency
Missing information or an external decision.
Completion evidence
What confirms the task is actually finished.

Use the log alongside the company’s actual requirements and agreed service scope. It does not replace an official filing calendar. Review notices and questions as they arrive and keep proof of responses. If activity or company details change, ask which updates are needed rather than assuming an old checklist continues to cover the new circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

Should I change company details every new year?

Only when a real change requires it; a calendar change alone is not a reason to rewrite the records.

What is a useful first review item?

Check that your current business description matches actual contracts, sales channels and payment flows.
