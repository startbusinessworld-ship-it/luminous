Keep the team informed of real changes

Explain new activities, counterparties or participant changes that affect the work requested. A briefing from before launch may no longer describe the company.

Maintain the evidence flow

Agree who supplies records and answers questions. Keep invoices, statements and company documents organised rather than forwarding fragments without context.

Review the scope periodically

Check which tasks are included and which require a separate instruction. Growth can change the support needed even when the company name remains the same.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.
