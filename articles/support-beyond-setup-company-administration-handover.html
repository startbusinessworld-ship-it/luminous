Support beyond setup: a practical company administration handover

A company handover should leave the owner able to find documents, understand responsibilities and respond to future requests. It is a working process rather than simply receiving a folder.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

Check the document handover

Make an index of issued certificates and other company records provided. Confirm access to the digital copies and any physical kit. Identify missing items and distinguish originals from presentation copies.

Agree the correspondence workflow

Record the contact for notices and questions. Decide who monitors deadlines and who provides information for filings. Keep an alternative contact route for absences or access problems.

Connect administration to trading

Put contracts, invoices and statements into an organised routine. Match records to the company that actually entered the transaction. Keep personal and company activity distinguishable.

Review changes and future needs

If the activity, participants or service providers change, ask what updates are required. Review the support arrangement when the business grows rather than assuming the original setup remains sufficient.

An administration action log

Task
The correspondence, record or filing question.
Responsible person
Who coordinates it and who supplies inputs.
Dependency
Missing information or an external decision.
Completion evidence
What confirms the task is actually finished.

Use the log alongside the company’s actual requirements and agreed service scope. It does not replace an official filing calendar. Review notices and questions as they arrive and keep proof of responses. If activity or company details change, ask which updates are needed rather than assuming an old checklist continues to cover the new circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

Is a green company kit the whole handover?

No. Access, an index and clear continuing responsibilities matter as well as physical documents.

Can I wait until year-end to organise records?

It is more practical to keep them as transactions occur, avoiding a later reconstruction exercise.
