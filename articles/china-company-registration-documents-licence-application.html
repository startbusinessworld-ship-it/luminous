China company registration documents: the application behind the licence

A mainland China business licence is the result of registration, not the whole application. The supporting file explains the investors, company arrangements, premises and proposed activity.

Start with the local registration route

The Beijing official guide describes online and service-window routes. Use the competent authority for the location where your company will be registered. A city-specific guide is a starting point, not proof that every locality uses an identical workflow.

Prepare the main document categories

The guide lists the application form, Articles of Association, investor identification or qualification documents, appointment documents and premises-related evidence. Additional approvals can apply to activities that require them.

Understand the business licence fields

Read the registered name, unified social credit code, address and business scope together. They describe the registered enterprise; they are not interchangeable with bank approval, work permission or evidence of every separate operational authorisation.

Keep the explanation tied to the actual project

Confirm the intended activity and location before requesting documents. Translation, authentication and other formalities should be checked for the actual investor and current local instructions. The pictured example is educational, not an issued client licence.

Confirm the current local instructions before collecting documents. The Beijing guide provides an official example of the main categories, not a guarantee of identical requirements throughout mainland China. Investor circumstances, activity and location matter. Keep application records distinct from the resulting business licence and from separate account or operational arrangements.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Hong Kong BR the same as a mainland business licence?

No. Hong Kong and mainland China have different registration systems and documents.

Is every foreign-invested company application identical?

No. Location, investor type and proposed activity can change the requirements.
