Foreign income requires a closer look

Hong Kong’s source-of-profits analysis is central. The FSIE regime can also apply to specified foreign income received by an entity in a multinational group. [5] [6]

Singapore tax-resident companies may qualify for exemptions on specified foreign dividends, branch profits and service income received in Singapore, subject to conditions. A headline tax rate is not an effective-tax calculation. [7]

How to choose

Map your customers, suppliers, team and actual operations. Then assess account eligibility, obligations where the business is carried on and the annual compliance budget. Neither place of incorporation guarantees worldwide tax exemption, account approval or immigration permission.
