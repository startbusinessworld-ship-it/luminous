Hong Kong setup timing and offshore tax conditions

Keep formation timing separate from tax treatment.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 16-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Keep formation timing separate from tax treatment. Luminos describes three to five working days as an indicative setup timeframe after receiving a complete file, subject to review and approval. It is not a guaranteed government deadline, bank-opening deadline or decision on offshore profits.

Keep the distinction clear

Hong Kong’s territorial approach focuses on the source of profits. Offshore treatment depends on the operations and applicable rules, including any relevant foreign-sourced income provisions, rather than an automatic company label. Hong Kong Inland Revenue Department: territorial source principle.

A practical example

A company may receive its incorporation documents before its account application has been reviewed. It may also need to establish the source of its profits separately when preparing its tax position. One completed process does not decide the others.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Confirm when the complete-file timeline starts.
  2. Allow separately for institutional checks.
  3. Assess the profit-generating activities before making tax assumptions.

Are overseas customers enough to establish offshore tax treatment?

No. The source analysis considers the operations generating profits and the applicable rules. Customer location alone does not establish an automatic exemption.
