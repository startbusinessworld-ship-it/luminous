Who handles questions about your Hong Kong company?

Company support works best when there is a clear contact and clear responsibilities.

By Luminos · 12 November 2026 · 17-second video

Read the practical guide ↓

The practical answer

Company support works best when there is a clear contact and clear responsibilities. Know where to send company questions, how documents are shared and which issues require a financial institution or another professional. A dedicated contact should make the process understandable without implying control over outside decisions.

Keep the distinction clear

Company setup and later operations are distinct. Confirm the registered-office and secretary arrangements, then plan the records and obligations that continue after incorporation. Hong Kong Companies Registry: incorporation.

A practical example

A founder receiving a request for updated company details can ask the support team to help identify the relevant records. If the request comes from an institution, that institution still decides whether the response meets its requirements.

This is an illustration of the process, not a promised outcome for an individual application.

What to prepare next

  1. Agree the main point of contact.
  2. Use the agreed channel for company documents.
  3. Clarify when specialist or institutional input is needed.

Does a dedicated contact replace the bank or tax authority?

No. The contact supports the process within the agreed service scope. Decisions and requirements of outside organisations remain separate.
