A clear business enquiry: what to tell Luminos before the first call

A clear enquiry tells the team where you are now and what help you need next. It does not require a long presentation or sensitive documents in an opening message.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article

  1. State the current stage
  2. Give the operating outline
  3. Describe the immediate issue
  4. Agree a secure next step
  5. A useful follow-up record
  6. Frequently asked questions

State the current stage

Say whether you are exploring an idea, preparing formation, running a company or answering a provider request. Include the relevant entity if one already exists. Do not make the team infer the stage from a screenshot.

Give the operating outline

Explain the offer, customer type, delivery method and countries involved. Mention an existing store or trading history accurately. Distinguish your business from an example you have seen online.

Describe the immediate issue

Identify the task that would help most now, such as understanding required company documents or organising a provider response. Include any actual request you are trying to answer, without exposing private information unnecessarily.

Agree a secure next step

Ask what information is needed and how to send it. Keep the response and action list together. For any outside approval, distinguish preparation from the final decision.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

Should my first message include a passport?

Do not send sensitive identification unnecessarily; confirm the purpose and secure route first.

Can I ask about several services?

Yes. List them separately and prioritise the immediate need so the scope stays clear.
