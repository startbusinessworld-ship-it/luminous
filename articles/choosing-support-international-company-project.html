Choosing personal support for an international company project

Personal support is useful when it makes responsibilities clear. Before choosing assistance, understand which tasks are included, which depend on outside authorities and what you must still provide.

By Luminos editorial team · 13 November 2026

In this article
  1. Define the scope in writing
  2. Agree how questions are handled
  3. Separate assistance from approval
  4. Keep the project understandable
  5. A useful follow-up record
  6. Frequently asked questions

Define the scope in writing

Separate company formation, local company functions, account preparation and continuing administration. Ask what each service produces and what is outside its scope. A broad promise to help does not answer those questions.

Agree how questions are handled

Choose a main contact and a secure route for sensitive information. Ask how missing items and third-party requests will be communicated. Keep important decisions in a written record.

Separate assistance from approval

An adviser may help prepare and coordinate a file, but a registrar, provider or other authority makes its own decisions. Reliable support should explain that boundary rather than promise an outcome it cannot control.

Keep the project understandable

After each discussion, record the next action, owner and any dependency. A short clear follow-up is often more valuable than a long exchange with no agreed result.

A useful follow-up record

Request
Write the precise task or question.
Input
List the information genuinely available.
Responsibility
Identify who supplies the answer or document.
Next status
Record what will confirm completion of this stage.

Use this record after a conversation, especially when several founders or advisers are involved. Keep one current version and attach important decisions. If the next outcome depends on a provider or authority, record that dependency instead of replacing it with an assumed completion date. Do not place passwords or private identification in a general action log.

Frequently asked questions

Can support guarantee a business account?

No. The provider retains its own assessment and approval process.

What should I ask before starting?

Ask for the scope, required inputs, communication method and the next concrete deliverable.
