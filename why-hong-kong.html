Hong Kong company formation in 2026: rules, costs and obligations

Updated 19 October 2026Luminos teamLire en français

A non-resident entrepreneur can form a Hong Kong private company limited by shares. This 2026 guide explains the main requirements, official charges and ongoing responsibilities before you choose a formation package.

What you need

At least one member, an individual director, a Hong Kong registered office and a local company secretary are required. The director need not live in Hong Kong; a sole director cannot also be the secretary. There is no statutory minimum paid-up capital. Filing includes NNC1, articles of association and IRBR1. [1]

Official charges in 2026

Electronic incorporation of a company with share capital costs HKD 1,545. For applications submitted from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027, a one-year Business Registration Certificate costs HKD 2,350, comprising a HKD 2,200 fee and HKD 150 levy. These two charges total HKD 3,895 before professional services. [1] [2]

Does offshore mean zero tax?

The source of trading profits depends on the operations that produce them and where those operations take place. Foreign customers alone do not establish offshore treatment. Keep evidence supporting the actual transactions and their location. [3]

Eligible corporations pay 8.25% on the first HKD 2 million of assessable profits and 16.5% above that under the two-tier regime. Only one connected entity can benefit within a group of connected entities. [4] [5]

FSIE rules separately address specified foreign interest, dividends, disposal gains and intellectual-property income received in Hong Kong by an entity in a multinational group. Exemption conditions depend on the income and circumstances. [6]

Ongoing responsibilities

An active private company normally files NAR1 within 42 days after its incorporation anniversary. Business registration renewal, accounts, audit and tax returns are separate tasks. Simplified reporting is not an audit exemption; legally dormant companies follow specific rules. [7] [8]
